AI Personality Design

Personality is not a prompt. It is a set of stable constraints.

A good character has boundaries: what they care about, how they react under pressure, and what they would never do. Those constraints are what make the persona feel real over hundreds of turns.

The temptation is to make every character agreeable. The memorable ones are the ones allowed to push back.

Constraints, not adjectives, are what create a believable character.
